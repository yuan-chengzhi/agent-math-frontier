# Agent working agreement

This repository is an evidence ledger, not a leaderboard of alleged AI solves.

Before promoting or attacking a problem:

1. Freeze the canonical statement, source revision, conventions, success
   criterion, partial-credit criterion, evaluator and budget.
2. Ask one independent reviewer to reconstruct the intended statement and a
   different reviewer to try to disprove that the problem is still open.
3. For a proof-assistant artifact, compile it and review statement fidelity as
   separate tasks. A compiling theorem is not evidence that the translation is
   faithful.
4. Red-team exact checkers for boundary errors and score exploits before a
   solver sees them.
5. Preserve failed attempts and costs. The solver may not be the final reviewer
   of correctness, novelty or significance.
6. Record conflicting evidence instead of silently overwriting old status.

Do not add a scalar "agent suitability" score. Keep the nine-dimensional
vector, hard gates and risks visible. Prefer upstream links and concise
paraphrases over copying problem statements.
